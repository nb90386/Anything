Pattern across all eight (INFERRED): in 2026 every workflow feature that a model can perform is already shipped by the incumbent that owns the data, the customer relationship, or the procurement vehicle, usually within months of the capability appearing. A solo founder does not win by building the same feature better. A solo founder wins where an incumbent structurally cannot follow: neutrality (a seller cannot credibly grade its own output), a measurement standard nobody owns, or a legal change that incumbents have not yet productized.

Alternative terminology searched by agents: "PDF remediation AI", "PDF/UA automation", "document accessibility AI 2026", "AI PDF remediation startup", "accessible agenda packet". International: EqualWeb (Israel), axes4 (Switzerland), Hancom (Korea) noted.

Weekend-copy test. Could a well-funded incumbent reproduce the verifier MVP in a weekend? Yes: the prompt and the tag-tree serialization are a few hundred lines. What does not copy in a weekend: (1) neutrality (an incumbent remediator grading itself is not credible to a liable buyer, the way an auditor cannot audit its own books); (2) a labeled corpus of real failures confirmed by blind screen-reader users; (3) a public benchmark that vendors are measured against; (4) the evidence trail entities accumulate. Of these, only (2) and (3) are real moats and both take months of unscalable work. If an incumbent with distribution (Siteimprove, Adobe) ships "semantic verification" first and is trusted, the neutral-verifier thesis weakens to a feature. This is the main competitive risk and is carried into 08 and 16.
