# Round 2 criteria (founder request, 2026-11-03)

Founder asked for: a bigger market, more impact, tested, worth daily hours, money coming faster, can be a business, "most viable and easily makes thousands a month".

Re-weighted scoring (100):
| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Speed to first $1k/month and $5k/month (buyers already spend money today on this outcome) | 25 |
| Evidence of existing spend and price (verified prices, RFPs, marketplace jobs) | 15 |
| Founder can sell and deliver it at 17 (no licensing, HIPAA BAAs, insurance or procurement walls for the first customers) | 15 |
| Market size (bottom-up) | 15 |
| Impact on people | 10 |
| Delivery cost advantage from AI (gross margin) | 10 |
| Defensibility / path to a bigger company | 5 |
| Legal and reputational risk (inverse) | 5 |

Hard rejects: needs a license or credential the founder lacks; needs HIPAA BAAs or regulated data for first revenue; generic AI agency with no specific outcome; buyer cannot pay within 60 days.
