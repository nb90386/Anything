**Subject: A contract-intelligence demo I built after studying Malbek's product space**

Hi [Name],

I'm a student looking for an internship, and I wanted to reach out with something more useful than a resume.

I spent the last stretch of time studying Malbek and the broader CLM market: BusinessIQ, the AI positioning, and
where products like Ironclad, Icertis, Sirion, and Agiloft already do well. Rather than just describe what I
learned, I built a working demo to prove I could turn that research into something real.

**Malbek Revenue Intelligence and Contract Risk Command Center [LINK]** is an independent portfolio project. It is
not affiliated with or endorsed by Malbek, and it is not a public release; I'm using Malbek's name and colors only
inside this private demo, with informal permission, because Malbek's product space is what inspired it. It runs
against twenty-five seeded contracts and, entirely offline with no API keys, it:

- Detects revenue leakage across a portfolio (missed escalators, expired renewals, payment-term mismatches) and
  traces every dollar figure back to a specific clause and contract
- Scores clause drift against a house playbook, so risk is measured against what Legal actually wants, not a
  generic severity label
- Answers natural-language questions about the whole portfolio, grounded in and citing the real underlying clauses
- Rolls all of it into role-based command centers for Legal, Sales, Finance, Procurement, and Executive views, plus
  a live, exportable board report

It's a full build: Next.js and TypeScript, a real SQLite data model, a deterministic rule-based analysis engine with
a clean interface to swap in Claude or GPT, and a Playwright/Vitest test suite. I also wrote up the market research
and product decisions behind it, happy to share if useful.

I'd welcome five minutes of feedback, or to talk about whether there's an internship fit at Malbek. Either way,
thank you for building something interesting enough to spend real time on.
