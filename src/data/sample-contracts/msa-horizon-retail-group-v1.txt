MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into by and between Northwind Analytics, Inc. ("Company") and Horizon Retail Group. ("Counterparty") as of September 1, 2024 (the "Effective Date").

1. DEFINITIONS

"Services" means the data analytics, dashboarding, and consulting services described in one or more Statements of Work executed under this Agreement. "Confidential Information" has the meaning set forth in Section 6.

2. TERM AND TERMINATION

This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of three (3) years. Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach if the breaching party fails to cure such breach within thirty (30) days after written notice describing the breach in reasonable detail. Upon termination, Company shall provide sixty (60) days of transition assistance at Counterparty's then-current rates.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT

Counterparty shall pay the fees set forth in each Statement of Work. Company shall remit the Annual Platform Fee invoice no later than September 15, 2026. Undisputed invoices are due within thirty (30) days of receipt. Late payments accrue interest at 1% per month, which the parties agree is a reasonable and customary rate.

4. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

Each party's aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid by Counterparty in the preceding twelve (12) months, except for breaches of Section 6 (Confidentiality), infringement of intellectual property rights, or a party's gross negligence or willful misconduct. Neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.

5. INDEMNIFICATION

Each party shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the other party from third-party claims arising from the indemnifying party's breach of this Agreement, negligence, or violation of applicable law, subject to the liability cap in Section 4.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

Each party shall protect the other's Confidential Information using the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, and in no case less than reasonable care. Confidentiality obligations survive for five (5) years following termination of this Agreement, except that trade secrets remain protected for as long as they qualify as trade secrets under applicable law.

7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

Each party retains all right, title, and interest in its pre-existing intellectual property. Work product created specifically for Counterparty under a Statement of Work, and paid for in full, shall be owned by Counterparty upon final payment, subject to Company's retained license to reuse general methodologies, tools, and know-how developed during performance.

8. SERVICE LEVEL COMMITMENTS

Company shall use commercially reasonable efforts to achieve 99.5% platform uptime, measured monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance windows communicated at least 48 hours in advance. Support tickets classified as Severity 1 shall receive an initial response within four (4) business hours.

9. RENEWAL

This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year terms unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

10. AUDIT RIGHTS

Company reserves audit rights with respect to Counterparty's use of the Services for compliance purposes and may exercise them upon fifteen (15) business days' prior written notice, no more than once per twelve-month period.

11. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The parties consent to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Wilmington, Delaware.

12. ASSIGNMENT

Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, not to be unreasonably withheld, except that either party may assign this Agreement in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all of its assets upon written notice.

13. FORCE MAJEURE

Neither party shall be liable for delay or failure to perform resulting from causes beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, labor disputes, or governmental action, provided the affected party gives prompt notice and uses reasonable efforts to mitigate.

14. QUARTERLY BUSINESS REVIEW

Counterparty shall submit a quarterly report on usage and satisfaction no later than October 1, 2026, and shall include roadmap priorities for the following quarter.
