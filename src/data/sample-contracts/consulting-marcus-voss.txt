INDEPENDENT CONSULTING AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into by and between Northwind Analytics, Inc. ("Company") and Marcus Voss Consulting LLC ("Counterparty") as of June 20, 2026 (the "Effective Date").

1. SERVICES

Counterparty shall provide fractional talent-acquisition advisory services to Company for approximately fifteen (15) hours per week, as directed by Company's VP of People.

2. ONBOARDING COMPLIANCE

Consultant shall submit an onboarding compliance report within thirty (30) days of the Effective Date, confirming completion of Company's security awareness and confidentiality training.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION

This Agreement continues for twelve (12) months from the Effective Date. Either party may terminate for convenience upon fifteen (15) days' written notice.

4. FEES

Company shall pay Counterparty a monthly retainer of $7,000, invoiced in arrears and due within fifteen (15) days of receipt.

5. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR STATUS

Counterparty is an independent contractor and not an employee, partner, or agent of Company. Counterparty is solely responsible for its own taxes, benefits, and insurance.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

Counterparty shall protect Company's confidential information using reasonable care and shall not disclose it during or after the engagement, except as required by law.

7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

All work product created by Counterparty in the course of this engagement is a work made for hire and shall be owned by Company upon creation, to the extent permitted by law, and Counterparty assigns all remaining right, title, and interest to Company.

8. NON-SOLICITATION

During the Term and for twelve (12) months thereafter, Counterparty shall not solicit Company's employees or clients with whom Counterparty had material contact during the engagement.

9. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

Each party's aggregate liability shall not exceed the total fees paid to Counterparty in the preceding twelve (12) months, except for confidentiality breaches.

10. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.
