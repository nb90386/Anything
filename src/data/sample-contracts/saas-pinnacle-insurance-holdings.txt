SAAS SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into by and between Northwind Analytics, Inc. ("Company") and Pinnacle Insurance Holdings ("Counterparty") as of May 1, 2025 (the "Effective Date").

1. SUBSCRIPTION AND ACCESS

Counterparty grants Company a non-exclusive, non-transferable subscription to access the Pinnacle claims-analytics platform (the "Platform") for the Term, subject to the terms of this Agreement and the applicable order form.

2. TERM AND TERMINATION

The initial term is twelve (12) months from the Effective Date. Either party may terminate for uncured material breach following thirty (30) days' written notice.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT

Company shall pay each invoice within ninety (90) days of receipt, reflecting Counterparty's extended Net 90 payment terms. Overdue invoices accrue interest at 1.5% per month until paid in full. All fees are non-refundable once the annual term has commenced.

4. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

Each party's aggregate liability shall not exceed the total fees paid by Company in the preceding twelve (12) months, except for confidentiality breaches, IP infringement, or willful misconduct.

5. INDEMNIFICATION

Each party shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the other party from third-party claims arising from the indemnifying party's breach, negligence, or violation of applicable law, subject to the liability cap in Section 4.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

Each party shall protect the other's confidential information using reasonable care and shall not disclose it to third parties without consent, except as required by law.

7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

The Platform, including all underlying software, algorithms, and documentation, is and remains the exclusive property of Counterparty. Company receives no ownership interest in the Platform or any derivative improvements.

8. SERVICE LEVEL COMMITMENTS

Counterparty will use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain 99.5% Platform availability, measured monthly, with service credits owed for shortfalls below the committed threshold.

9. AUTO-RENEWAL

This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month terms at Counterparty's then-current list price unless Company provides notice at least sixty (60) days before the renewal date. Company must deliver written notice of non-renewal no later than March 1, 2026 to prevent automatic renewal for an additional twelve (12) month term.

10. DATA HANDLING

Company retains ownership of all data uploaded to the Platform. Counterparty shall delete Company data within thirty (30) days of termination upon written request.

11. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Connecticut.

12. ASSIGNMENT

Counterparty may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of Company to any affiliate or successor entity in connection with a corporate reorganization.
