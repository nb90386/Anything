RESELLER AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into by and between Northwind Analytics, Inc. ("Company") and Solstice Hardware Partners ("Counterparty") as of March 1, 2024 (the "Effective Date").

1. APPOINTMENT

Company appoints Counterparty as a non-exclusive reseller of Company's analytics products in the territory described in Exhibit A, and Counterparty accepts such appointment subject to the terms of this Agreement.

2. TERM AND TERMINATION

This Agreement has an initial term of twelve (12) months from the Effective Date. Either party may terminate for uncured material breach following thirty (30) days' written notice.

3. PRICING AND PAYMENT

Counterparty shall purchase products at the wholesale price schedule set forth in Exhibit B and resell at prices of its choosing. Reseller shall remit the final True-Up payment no later than February 1, 2025.

4. NON-COMPETE

During the Term and for five (5) years thereafter, Reseller shall not engage in any business anywhere in the world that competes with Company's Products, including development, marketing, or distribution of competing analytics platforms.

5. MARKETING OBLIGATIONS

Counterparty shall use commercially reasonable efforts to market and promote Company's products, including maintaining a trained sales staff and participating in joint marketing programs as mutually agreed.

6. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

Each party's aggregate liability shall not exceed the total fees paid by Counterparty in the preceding twelve (12) months, except for breaches of Section 4 (Non-Compete) or confidentiality obligations.

7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

Counterparty is granted a limited, non-exclusive license to use Company's trademarks solely in connection with marketing and reselling Company's products during the Term.

8. CONFIDENTIALITY

Each party shall protect the other's confidential and proprietary information using reasonable care and shall not disclose it to third parties without written consent.

9. WARRANTY

Company warrants that its products will perform substantially in accordance with their published documentation for ninety (90) days from delivery.

10. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Arizona.

11. ASSIGNMENT

Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, except in connection with a merger or sale of substantially all assets.
