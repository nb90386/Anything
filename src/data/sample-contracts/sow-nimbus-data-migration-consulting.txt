STATEMENT OF WORK - DATA MIGRATION CONSULTING

This Statement of Work is entered into by and between Northwind Analytics, Inc. ("Company") and Nimbus Data Systems ("Counterparty") as of April 1, 2026 (the "Effective Date").

1. SCOPE OF WORK

Counterparty shall provide consulting services to migrate Company's legacy infrastructure data into the Nimbus managed platform, including data mapping, cleansing, transformation, and validation across three (3) source systems.

2. DELIVERABLES AND MILESTONES

Counterparty shall deliver Milestone 1 (Data Mapping) no later than September 1, 2026. Milestone 2 (Migration and Validation) shall be delivered within sixty (60) days of Milestone 1 acceptance.

3. FEES

The total fixed fee for this Statement of Work is $210,000. The mobilization fee is non-refundable prior to completion of Milestone 1 and is due no later than April 20, 2026. The remaining balance is due upon final acceptance.

4. ACCEPTANCE

Company shall have ten (10) business days to review each deliverable and either accept it or provide written feedback describing any deficiencies, which Counterparty shall remedy before resubmission.

5. CHANGE MANAGEMENT

Any changes to scope, timeline, or fees must be documented in a written change order signed by both parties before Counterparty is obligated to perform the additional work.

6. TERM

This Statement of Work commences on the Effective Date and continues until all deliverables are accepted, currently estimated to conclude by December 1, 2026.

7. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

Each party's aggregate liability shall not exceed the total fees paid under this Statement of Work, except for confidentiality breaches or willful misconduct.

8. CONFIDENTIALITY

Each party shall protect the other's confidential technical information using reasonable care and shall not disclose it without written consent.

9. GOVERNING LAW

This Statement of Work is governed by the laws of the State of Washington.
