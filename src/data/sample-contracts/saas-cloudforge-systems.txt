SAAS SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into by and between Northwind Analytics, Inc. ("Company") and CloudForge Systems ("Counterparty") as of August 1, 2025 (the "Effective Date").

1. SUBSCRIPTION AND ACCESS

Counterparty grants Company a non-exclusive, non-transferable subscription to access the CloudForge data-warehousing platform (the "Platform") for the Term, subject to the terms of this Agreement and the applicable order form.

2. TERM AND TERMINATION

The initial term is twelve (12) months from the Effective Date. Counterparty may terminate this Agreement in its sole discretion and without cause at any time upon thirty (30) days' notice to Company, and may additionally terminate immediately upon written notice for any suspected violation of its acceptable use policy, in Counterparty's sole judgment.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT

Company shall pay the Annual Subscription Fee invoice no later than July 25, 2026. Overdue invoices accrue interest at 1.5% per month until paid in full. All fees are non-refundable once the annual term has commenced.

4. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

Counterparty's liability shall not be limited in any respect arising from Company's use of the Platform, including for outages, data loss, or service interruptions, except as expressly required by applicable law.

5. INDEMNIFICATION

Company shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Counterparty from any and all claims, regardless of fault, arising from Company's use of the Platform or breach of this Agreement.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY

Each party shall protect the other's confidential information using reasonable care and shall not disclose it to third parties without consent, except as required by law.

7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

The Platform, including all underlying software, algorithms, and documentation, is and remains the exclusive property of Counterparty. Company receives no ownership interest in the Platform or any derivative improvements.

8. SERVICE LEVEL COMMITMENTS

Counterparty will use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain Platform availability but makes no specific uptime commitment and disclaims all warranties, express or implied, including merchantability and fitness for a particular purpose.

9. AUTO-RENEWAL

This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month terms at Counterparty's then-current list price unless Company provides notice at least ninety (90) days before the renewal date. Company must deliver written notice of non-renewal no later than May 3, 2026 to prevent automatic renewal for an additional twelve (12) month term.

10. DATA HANDLING

Company retains ownership of all data uploaded to the Platform. Counterparty shall delete Company data within thirty (30) days of termination upon written request.

11. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas. Disputes shall be resolved through binding arbitration in Austin, Texas.

12. ASSIGNMENT

Counterparty may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of Company to any affiliate or successor entity in connection with a corporate reorganization.
